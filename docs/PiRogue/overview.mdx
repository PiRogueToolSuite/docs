# Overview

<div style={{textAlign: 'center', margin: '1.5rem 0'}}>
  <img src="/img/logos/pirogue.svg" alt="PiRogue logo" style={{width: '120px', height: '120px'}} />
</div>

The PiRogue is a Raspberry Pi-based network router that captures and analyzes network traffic in real time. It is the foundation of the PiRogue Tool Suite, and it can also run on a regular computer or a server.

You connect the device you want to examine to the PiRogue, and the PiRogue shows you what that device talks to. It is used for:

* **Network traffic analysis**: deep packet inspection of the traffic to spot suspicious patterns and potential threats
* **Mobile device forensics**: the consensual extraction and analysis of the data of a mobile device, such as messages and application data, to reveal user activity or signs of compromise
* **Mobile app analysis**: recording what an app sends, even when the traffic is encrypted
